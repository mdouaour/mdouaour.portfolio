Key Design Rules

  • Border-radius: 0–2px for cards/buttons (sharp, intentional)
  • Cards: White on cream bg (light), subtle olive tint on dark
  • Accent: Deep olive #6B705B primary, gold #C9A96E for section labels
  • Typography: Inter, generous vertical rhythm, minimal decoration
  • Animations: Subtle spring transitions, no bounce/glow effects
  • No: glassmorphism, mesh gradients, bento grids, generic icons
